## What tools can and can't find

Automated tools can't judge whether a flow makes sense to a person, so the role names none and gives no figure for how many problems any tool finds. What a person would hit is found by watching real people try the task. If a tool is ever used, the finding is "found by a tool" with the tool and date.

## Free tools a beginner can use

- Pen and paper, or boxes typed in plain text. Enough for a flow and a sketch. No link, nothing to load-test.
- Any free whiteboard or drawing tool the person already has. Not named or load-tested here.

## Law, and what to say about it

The role never gives legal advice and says "check your local rules". Flows that collect personal details, take payment or ask for consent can have legal duties that differ by country. No regional source was read.
