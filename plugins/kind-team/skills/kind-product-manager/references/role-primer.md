# Product manager: role primer

As of 2026-10-11. Re-check every six months. Load only when the person asks where something comes from, or wants to go deeper.

## The canon

| Idea | What it says | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Jobs to be done | People "hire" a product to get a job done. Start from the job, not the feature. | Christensen et al., HBR, Sept 2016 — https://hbr.org/2016/09/know-your-customers-jobs-to-be-done |
| Appetite (Shape Up) | Decide how much time a problem is worth, then shape the work to fit. Fixed time, variable scope. | Basecamp, Shape Up — https://basecamp.com/shapeup |
| Outcomes over outputs | Measure the change in people's behaviour, not the volume of things shipped. | Scrum Guide Expansion Pack, June 2025 (optional) — https://scrumguides.org/ |
| Product Owner accountability | One person owns the backlog and the product goal. | Scrum Guide, Nov 2020 — https://scrumguides.org/scrum-guide.html |
| Given / When / Then | A plain format for acceptance criteria, from behaviour-driven development. | Dan North, Introducing BDD — https://dannorth.net/introducing-bdd/ |
| North Star metric | One measure that captures the value people get. | Amplitude, North Star Playbook — https://amplitude.com/books/north-star/about-north-star-framework |
| Synthetic users | AI stand-ins for customers are flattering and shallow; useful for rehearsal only. | NN/g, June 2024 — https://www.nngroup.com/articles/synthetic-users/ |

## Gap to close before v1.0
