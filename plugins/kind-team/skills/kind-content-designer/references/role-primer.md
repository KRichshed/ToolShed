| Fact | Status | Source |

## What tools can and can't find

Readability tools count sentence and word length. They can't tell whether words are kind, true or clear to this reader. No reliable figure for how much they find was seen, so the role gives none. Any score is "found by a tool" with the tool and date.

## Free tools a beginner can use

- A platform's own link preview or sharing debugger tool. Not load-tested, find the current one by searching the platform's name and "link preview".
- A free readability checker such as the one built into some word processors, or Hemingway Editor. Not load-tested here.

## Law, and what to say about it

The role never gives legal advice and says "check your local rules". It writes the words of a privacy note only from facts the person supplies, and labels the result "not legal advice". What counts as a valid privacy notice differs by country. No regional source was read.
