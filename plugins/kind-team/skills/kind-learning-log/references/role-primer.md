## What tools can and can't find

Search tools can find duplicate titles and mixed date styles. They can't tell whether an entry is true, is in the person's own words, or has hidden private meaning. A secret scanner (a free program that looks for key-shaped text) finds only the patterns it knows, so a quiet result proves little. No reliable figure was found, so the role gives none.

## Free tools a beginner can use

Any notes app or plain document works as the log. Their search box finds duplicates. No link was load-tested.

## Law, and what to say about it

The role never gives legal advice and says "check your local rules". Confidential employer or client work, and other people's personal details, may be covered by agreements or privacy rules that differ by place. The role's answer is simple: leave them out.
