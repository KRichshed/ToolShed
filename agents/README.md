# Agents

Role setups and multi-step workflows that combine skills for larger jobs.

## How agents fit into the shed

An **agent** is the third step on the ladder:

1. **Prompt ([`prompts/`](../prompts/)):** A single question you ask once.
2. **Skill ([`skills/`](../skills/)):** A reusable guide (`SKILL.md`) that teaches your AI tool one repeatable practice.
3. **Agent (`agents/`):** A role or workflow that combines several skills across steps, pausing for your review at each checkpoint.
